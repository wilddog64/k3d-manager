## Why

The Fable weekly limit is a gauge pair (used ratio plus a neutral "resets at" gauge) on **Agent Cost**, row "Fable". The
Codex and Claude weekly and 5-hour limits are plain stats on **Agent Activity**, with no reset time, although both reset
times are already exported (`k3dm_codex_quota_resets_at_seconds`, `bin/k3dm-dispatch-metrics:2173`;
`k3dm_claude_quota_resets_at_seconds`, `:2205`). All plan limits belong in one place, in one form.
