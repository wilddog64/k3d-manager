# Prompts by model, sender and cause

**Status:** PLANNED — not dispatched. Moved out of P0 S1 on 2026-11-06 on the second review's advice (`docs/issues/2026-11-06-s1-spec-second-review-findings.md`): it is a separate analytics contract and S1's gate replay should settle first. Starts after S1 lands, because both edit the same files.
**Approval:** explicit 2026-11-06 "go ahead" (operator, after asking "do we know which vendor/model need more human invervention?"). Dispatch still needs its go as a hub dashboard spec.
**Priority:** P2
**Branch:** `k3d-manager-v1.44.0`

## Why

The Agent Dispatch panel counts prompts sent to workers, but cannot say which model needed them, who wrote them, or whether the spec or the worker was the cause. As of 2026-11-06 Luna had 74 tasks and every other named model one, so the comparison the operator asked for cannot be made yet. Recording this now means the data exists when more models have run routine work.

## Fix

### Dispatcher

`intervention` and `resume` gain optional `--sent-by operator|coordinator` and `--cause spec|worker|environment|unknown`. `_dispatch_resume` passes them to the `intervention` it records. They are stored as `sent_by` and `cause` on the `intervention` line only; a flag not supplied writes no field. A value outside those lists exits 2 before anything is written and before a worker starts. They are optional so nothing is blocked, and with neither flag the lines are the same as before. `make codex-resume` passes `SENT_BY=` and `CAUSE=`. `docs/howto/codex-dispatch.md` says to always pass both, and that `sent_by` is who wrote the prompt, not who ran the command.

### Attribution

Replay each `(version, slug)` in line order over **all** its lines, including those before the coverage marker and outside the window, to establish the current model; only then filter what is counted. The current model starts as `unknown`. At each `start` and `resume` it becomes `worker_identity.model` if that is a non-empty string other than `unknown`; otherwise `model` under the same test; otherwise it keeps its value. An `intervention` is charged to the current model at its line. `bin/k3dm-codex-dispatch` records the `intervention` just before its `resume`, so a prompt that switches model is charged to the model whose work prompted it, not the replacement.

### Series

In the same block and over the same `scoped` events as `k3dm_dispatch_human_continuation_prompts`, and only when that series is emitted:

- `k3dm_dispatch_model_prompts{window,model,reason,sent_by,cause}`: one row per non-zero combination, for the four reasons that series counts, regardless of the marker. Its sum by `reason`, in every window, equals that series.
- `k3dm_dispatch_model_tasks{window,model}`: distinct `(version, slug)` pairs that were charged a counted prompt in scope, regardless of the marker, or where the model was current at a `start` or `resume` in scope with `ts` at or after the marker. The marker is the earliest `intervention_coverage_start` by `ts`, as the existing series reads it. So every counted prompt has a task, and the denominator cannot be zero.
- `k3dm_dispatch_model_prompts_per_task{window,model}`: prompts divided by tasks, only when tasks is 10 or more, including when the answer is 0. Below 10 it is absent, because a ratio over a few tasks reads as a ranking it cannot support.

`sent_by` and `cause` export the recorded value, or `unrecorded` when absent or outside the schema; nothing is inferred.

**Bounding `model`, in this order, per window.** A name failing `^[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9._-]{0,63}$`, or literally `other`, goes to `other`. The remaining names are ranked by distinct tasks, descending, ties by name ascending; the first 12 keep their name and the rest go to `other`. `other` is then the union: tasks counted distinct, so a task touching two merged models counts once, and prompts summed. The ratio limit applies after merging.

### Panels

Agent Dispatch gains two tables driven by `$window`: **Prompts by model** (model, tasks, prompts, per task, joined by model so a model with tasks and no prompts still has a row) and **Prompts by sender and cause** (`sent_by`, `cause`, reason, prompts). Their descriptions say a model that handled harder tasks is not thereby a worse model, that per task appears only from 10 tasks, and that `unrecorded` means no sender or cause was recorded. The existing panel keeps its title **Human continuation prompts**, and its description gains: "Most of these prompts are written by the coordinator, not a person; see Prompts by sender and cause."

### Fixtures (committed)

`scripts/tests/fixtures/dispatch-prompts/prompts.jsonl` and `prompts-expected.json`, read with `render(events, 0, now=<now in the expected file>)` so no result depends on the clock. Cases: a model switch, the `model` fallback, no model, a resume keeping the model, a start before the marker, a prompt before the marker, a task crossing the marker, a reason outside the four, a bad model name, a bad sender and cause, a prompt with no start, and tasks either side of the 10-task limit.

## What NOT to Do

- Do NOT infer a sender or cause from the prompt or its reason.
- Do NOT change `k3dm_dispatch_human_continuation_prompts` or any other existing series.
- Do NOT add a slug label.
- Do NOT rank or colour models as better or worse.
- Do NOT commit, push or create a PR. Claude reviews and commits.
