## Purpose

To find out cheaply whether the 30-day run is worth doing, and to shake out the harness. The pilot is not
scored as pass/fail on the question itself.

## Plan

- 5 days, weekdays, full traffic and change load.
- Chaos is **not** sealed in the pilot. Each catalog fault is injected and aborted once, on purpose,
  while the operator watches (evidence E3 of plan 3). One sealed fault on day 4 or 5 tests the
  scheduler itself.
- Daily: the operator logs minutes, and Claude runs `score` and files a `docs/bugs/` entry for each harness
  defect.

## Go / no-go for v1.64.0

Go when all hold:
- the harness ran 5 days without a defect that corrupts a measurement;
- every fault reversed cleanly, by hand and by abort;
- the off-hub probe saw every outage the hub's own monitoring saw;
- operator time averaged under 90 min/day. The pilot allows slack for harness work; the run's limit
  stays 60.

No-go writes down why and reschedules v1.64.0. A no-go is a valid finding, not a failure of the plan.

## Operator decisions (decided 2026-11-07: "go for your recommendation")

1. **Hub:** stays on the laptop, as today. Hub fragility is part of the question, and physical outages count against the SLO.
2. **Platform releases:** keep merging during the pilot and the run. Each merge is recorded as change load.
3. **Hostinger:** probe-only. It receives the external read-only probes and never a chaos fault, and the chaos allowlist excludes it.
