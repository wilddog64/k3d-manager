## What Went Well

- Five plans landed. Hub deploy-ref (`hub-deploy-ref-and-live-write-guard`) and `intent-verification` are LIVE-VERIFIED (2026-11-10: `deploy/hub` at `74cd3ca1`, 21 appset references on `deploy/hub`; operator approved the C1 four-part report).
- Every plan got an independent verifier from its Acceptance block only (2026-11-10/11), with a pre-fix red and mutants where recorded. The ledger holds a `verify` pass for each of the five.
- About 57 bugs were fixed in the release (2 P1), most through Luna or Flash 3.8 dispatch with a review child on the riskier ones.
- The capture-first rule (2026-11-11) kept P2/P3 from piling onto the release PR. The second-agent classification worked on 2026-11-12: Argon agreed with Claude's P2 on the Fable-found deploy/hub bug.
- A Fable pre-merge review found two P2s in the deploy/hub path before merge, not after (`docs/bugs/2026-11-12-hub-checkout-stops-following-deploy-hub-and-promote-guard-fails-open.md`), both reproduced in scratch repos.
- Copilot's one finding was valid and was triaged properly: the `eval` in `read_lines` was real, and the fix was written against bash 3.2 instead of taking the suggestion (see below).

## What Went Wrong

- Three plans merged without live proof. `checkout-auto-sync`, `control-node-linux` and `cost-attribution-coverage` passed offline verification only. `control-node-linux` E3 failed on a test-hermeticity P3 (`docs/bugs/2026-11-10-make-test-fails-on-fresh-linux-control-clone.md`; 2033 ok, 7 not ok); E4 Hostinger was not run. `cost-attribution-coverage` E3 never ran. `checkout-auto-sync` was exercised live in the 2026-11-12 promote, but nobody recorded it, so its plan still says "Live check pending".
- Escapes: 26 bug docs name v1.43.15 as the origin, and 28 match a v1.43.15 plan, commit or slug in a looser grep. Hub deploy-ref alone has 9 (`897cfe72`), checkout-auto-sync 5, control-node-linux 4. The Contract readers and Writers-too rules were added after this plan missed readers (`sensors.py:114`) and the CVE promoter writer (`app-cve-scan.sh:514-591`).
- Claude ran an unstubbed `make install-repo-sync` on the M4 during this release; the install's label-scoped bootout unloaded the live repo-sync agent and `K3dmRepoSyncStale` emailed the operator. Recorded as memory `feedback_never_run_install_targets_unstubbed` (stub launchctl; scratch HOME is not enough), not as a bug doc.
- A Flash 3.8 worker's test, in the repo-sync PATH P2 correction round (2026-11-11), ran `git config --global` without exporting a scratch HOME and overwrote the operator's M4 `~/.gitconfig` with a test identity. The land fast gate caught it; the operator restored the file and Claude verified it. Round 4 fenced the identity into the worktree's `.git/config` and added a `~/.gitconfig` hash guard. No separate bug doc.
- Copilot's suggested `read_lines` fix would have broken macOS `/bin/bash` 3.2.57 (the branch needs bash 4.1). Reproduced on the M4 on 2026-11-12 (`docs/issues/2026-11-12-copilot-pr160-review-findings.md`).
- Spec-then-fix churn on the hub-promote work: `hub-promote-keeps-promoter-commits` took four rounds (r1 to r4, Flash/Flash/Luna/Flash); `control-linux-vagrant-m4` three (all Flash). Detail in Dispatch Review.
- Review children were left open (three listed under Dispatch Review).
- Bug volume: 98 bug docs are dated 2026-11-07 to 2026-11-12; `docs/process-metrics.md` counts 77 filed for v1.43.15 as of 2026-11-11 (2 P1, 33 P2, 42 P3).
